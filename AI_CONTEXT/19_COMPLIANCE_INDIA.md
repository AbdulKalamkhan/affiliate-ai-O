# 19_COMPLIANCE_INDIA.md

**Not legal/tax advice — flag both sections below for review by a CA/lawyer before real money or external users are involved.**

## Tax (Owner's own business)
- Affiliate commission and seller income is taxable business/professional income under Indian tax law. GST registration relevance depends on turnover thresholds and whether commissions count as a taxable supply of service.
- `profit_records` should retain date, amount, and source — bookkeeping evidence only.
- This system must never auto-file or auto-remit tax — record-keeping only, human handles filing.

## Data protection (once external SaaS users exist — Phase 11+)
- India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023** applies to personal data of Indian users once external customers exist.
- Before Phase 11 multi-tenant work begins: implement explicit consent capture, a data-deletion mechanism, and a documented data-retention policy.
- Never store more customer data than the feature requires.
