**Project:** AI_OS — Affiliate AI CEO & Money Operating System
**Owner:** Nzb

**Philosophy:** Local-first, zero-budget/free-first, human-controlled, additive architecture

## One-line definition
A controlled AI-powered commerce operating system that finds, validates, scores, and executes profitable Seller + Affiliate + Social opportunities, measures real outcomes, learns, and compounds — with the Owner as final authority on every sensitive action.

## Business engines
1. **Seller Engine** — Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, Meesho Supplier Panel listings, inventory, orders, settlement.
2. **Affiliate Engine** — Amazon Associates (initially), affiliate links, click/conversion/commission tracking.
3. **Social Media Layer** — Owner-controlled YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest — content prepared by AI, published only after approval.

## Non-goals
Not an unrestricted autonomous agent, not a credential dumper, not an unauthorized scraper, not a spam engine, not a fake-review generator, not a guaranteed-income machine, not dependent on paid APIs, not merely a chatbot.
